# Portfolio
Portfolio for Mainframe Jobs

I want to create a modern, professional, and responsive personal portfolio website based on my attached resume.

The main purpose of this portfolio is to apply for new job opportunities, showcase my professional experience, technical skills, projects, and achievements, and make a strong impression on recruiters and hiring managers.

Please analyze my attached resume carefully and use the information from it to create the website content and structure.

The portfolio should include:

Hero section with my name, professional title, short introduction, and clear call-to-action buttons such as View Resume and Contact Me
About Me section with a concise professional summary
Technical Skills organized clearly by technology/category
Professional Experience with company, role, responsibilities, achievements, and technologies used
Projects highlighting the projects mentioned in my resume, my role, key contributions, challenges solved, and technologies used
Key Achievements section emphasizing measurable or important accomplishments
Education and Certifications
Contact section with email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and other relevant professional links available in my resume

Since my background is primarily in mainframe development, give appropriate prominence to technologies such as PL/I, COBOL, DB2, JCL, z/OS, and other tools mentioned in my resume.

Design requirements
Modern and premium developer-portfolio appearance
Clean, professional UI suitable for recruiters
Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile
Professional color palette with good typography
Smooth scrolling and subtle animations
Easy-to-scan experience and project sections
Navigation bar linking to major sections
Visually attractive skill cards/tags and project cards
Professional timeline for work experience
Download/View Resume button
Avoid unnecessary animations, clutter, and overly flashy design
Content requirements
Use only factual information available in my resume.
Do not invent experience, technologies, achievements, metrics, certifications, project details, or employers.
Rewrite resume content into concise, professional, recruiter-friendly website copy instead of simply copying the resume word-for-word.
Highlight my strongest technical skills and accomplishments.
Make the content ATS-friendly and keyword-rich while keeping it natural and human.
Keep confidential/client-sensitive information appropriately professional.

Make the final portfolio look like an experienced software developer's portfolio rather than a simple online resume.
